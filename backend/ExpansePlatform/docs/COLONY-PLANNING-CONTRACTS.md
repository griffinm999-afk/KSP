# Colony founding, reserves and growth

The selected save owns `Plans` and `ReorderPolicies`. The pure domain planner creates one reviewed itemized founding/growth quote, reserves its future cash, finite supplier quantities, actual owned materials and surveyed plots, then converts at most one commitment into an existing logistics/import/construction operation per pump. KSP's main-thread colony loop owns the scheduler; no desktop/Host scheduler owns these plans.

## Product commands and review

- `QuoteFoundingPlan(state,colonyId,env)` and `QuoteGrowthPlan(...)` return `ColonyPlanningQuote`: named building roles/packages, actual BOM plus embedded contents, plot identities, dependencies, labor, build escrow, individual freight loads, startup support, full funds total, preserved assets, blockers, downside forecast and concrete named bootstrap worker shifts.
- Product founding uses the typed overload `QuoteFoundingPlan(state,colonyId,env,intent)`. `ColonyFoundingIntent` resolves exact ordinary visitor designations, explicitly chosen paid new arrivals and optional `ColonyStartupIntent`; approval sends the resolved named intent with the exact quote. Null retains historical building-only paid contracts.
- `approveFoundingPlan` / `approveGrowthPlan` require the current `QuoteId`, colony ID, context, expected revision and ordinary operation ID. The adapter revalidates all terms. The immutable purchase quote remains saved separately from progress.
- `surveyFoundingPlan` / `surveyGrowthPlan` perform bounded actual loaded terrain/hardware surveys before recording plots. An unloaded geometric sketch cannot approve a plan. Placement performs its own final native checks.
- `cancelColonyPlan` releases future funds, supplier/material/plot claims and its extra uncommissioned support floor. Already paid/departed/construction child operations retain their saved terms and continue independently.
- `configureReorderPolicy` fields are `Resource`, `Enabled`, `ReorderPointMicroUnits`, `TargetMicroUnits`, `CadenceSeconds`. UI displays ordinary units; the ledger uses millionths.

`ColonyPlanning.cfg` explicitly maps package IDs to housing/workshop/storage/power/lamp roles, a six-hour review cadence and a 2× import delay stress model. Candidate templates require both the isolated authorized development environment and a sandbox charter. Normal career candidates require actual certification and unlocks.

Reviewed authority binds world/site/charter, receiving capacities/support floors, adopted vessel/part identities and stable installed provider/member identities plus current role qualifications. It excludes surplus stock consumption and dynamic utility temperature/timestamp evidence. Approval regenerates the entire current bill and allocations, including actual accessible owned inventory, promised cargo, supplier terms/availability, funds and fleet constraints; changed shortage, cost, allocation, member/provider identity or lost qualification requires review again. Saved paid quotes retain their original stored authority/hash and are never rewritten during migration.

## Reviewed startup stock and recurring policies

Support covers the distinct union of actual people, existing residents and named planned immigrants. Designating a current visitor does not add another person. Every named paid fare joins the same saved future-funds commitment as construction, staging and the maximum import bill; a child replaces its own future commitment before reserving the real fare effect, avoiding double reservation. Unknown paid/crew outcomes retain their exact roster/home claims even after parent cancellation.

`StartupPolicies.StockTargets` exposes initial Supplies and explicitly chosen Machinery, MaterialKits and EnrichedUranium buffers. Zero service quantities decline those buffers; no usage rate is inferred from a resource name or prefab. `ReserveMaterials` is the net operating quantity above protected support floors. `PlanningMaterialRequirements` combines this with the real building bill into the existing central plan claims and incoming-cargo assignments. Stock is never credited from a quote. Once all actual buildings and reviewed workers are operational, exact owned operating reserves are released for use, with a durable progress flag.

An enabled Supplies zero point derives `support floor + distinct promised population × support balance × maximum actual finite Supplies supplier transit × import-delay factor`. Zero target adds one review-cadence consumption. Explicit enabled levels below this runway hold with the exact minimum. For 23 present people, two named immigrants, six reserve days, three-day freight and 2× delay, the reviewed floor is 150, default point 300 and initial target 325 ordinary units. This is purchased stock and declared support balance, not native manufacturing output.

After real support commissioning, deterministic children apply the reviewed policies **before passenger dispatch and waiting**, allowing replenishment during travel. `Reorders` contains exact resource/enabled/point/target/cadence rows: Supplies uses its reviewed levels; each nonzero service buffer uses point=target and the reviewed service cadence, enabled only when service is enabled. Installed Machinery/ReplacementParts/EnrichedUranium service retains real qualified worker and exact tank/provider requirements. Local procurement binds the reviewed five-second, 1,000-unit, 50% native-input-buffer bounds. All recurring purchases use the existing cash floor, charter founding budget/spending limit, finite suppliers and globally shared freight pool. Parent completion still requires every named designation/arrival receipt; policies do not fabricate people or service stock.

New empty resident/intent/policy terms are omitted from canonical quote projection. Old paid quotes retain their exact hash and original import maximum; additive progress fields never rewrite the immutable bill. `MakeImportComparisons` is read-only: observed accessible warehouse quantity, itemized full import cost, cargo mass/volume and an explicit serial delayed-transit scenario. Unknown provider/funding holds have no finite completion guarantee. Investment payback remains explicitly unsupported without a qualified physical output-rate/input/labor/power contract; WOLF capacity points are never presented as commodity production throughput.

`BootstrapWorkers` binds each new worker-dependent building to a named actual ordinary visitor, trait, registered source cabin/vessel/PID and a maximum 200 m surface shift. Only transferable non-work cabins are selected; protected mission crew, existing workers, residents and already claimed people are excluded. Approval reserves the roster identity centrally. After actual placement the planner resolves a genuine WorkSupported cabin in that exact construction facility and queues a deterministic child through the shared worker-transfer provider. The child's actual source/destination occupancy quote and durable before/after witness are required; no house/resident label is needed to staff commissioning. The worker remains a visitor with existing home/resident labels preserved. Changed named source, unavailable cabin, range or worker qualification holds; no alternate crew are selected.

`Plans.Workers` records planned/queued/complete/cancelled claims. Own-child exclusion applies only to that exact deterministic operation and registered construction facility/work PID. Other people commands cannot consume a planned or uncertain worker claim. Cancellation releases unused planned workers; started/unknown children retain their crew ownership/witnesses. Referenced terminal PeopleOperations survive compaction for immutable plan lineage; if the bounded operation ledger has no discardable records, it visibly holds rather than erasing proof. Empty additive worker contracts preserve original pre-worker paid quote hashes.

## Conservation and chronology

Future cash lives in `RemainingFunds`; negative prepared effects replace that reservation before actual payment, never add a duplicate obligation. The shared cash guard includes future plans and all other prepared effects. Charter spending/founding budgets and cash floor remain enforced. Finite supplier stock has actual shipment reservations plus future plan claims. A second colony cannot recreate supplier inventory or enlarge the shared fleet.

Actual material claims increase `Stock.Reserved`. Cargo already on a route can be assigned once to a plan. On real chronological arrival, the arrival transition atomically transfers its material claim to owned stock before a user/worker could take it. Support stock is protected by its floor, rather than reserved as construction material. Before a building starts, plan ownership transfers to existing construction material/escrow reservations. Dependencies must be operational before dependent construction starts. Native labor/placement/commissioning contracts remain authoritative; awaiting placement is not operational housing.

Each child operation uses a deterministic ID from parent plan and line. The state stores child IDs, exact original price/travel/package/plot terms, immutable quote hash and quantities. Reload validation checks future-funding arithmetic, material ownership, cargo assignment, supplier bounds, plot ownership and child lineage. Unknown external effects stop planner/policy mutations. A changed supplier quote waits visibly; it cannot silently charge another price. Safe recovery is cancellation of future scope followed by a fresh reviewed plan, preserving already paid child operations.

## Forecast and growth assumptions

`Forecast` separates observed cash, committed cash, available cash after the shared floor and in-transit export receivables. The downside case spends zero future export receipts. It counts only owned supplies and delayed cargo arriving before stock would run out, quotes finite replacement input costs, and includes charter/person demand. Native physical/MKS/WOLF observations do not create owned stock or modeled output. Make-versus-import is explicit: owned stock first, finite imports for the remaining shortage; physical production requires a witnessed transfer before it becomes spendable.

Growth requires commissioned support, genuine housing pressure, actual qualified operational job vacancies, reliable current home utility witnesses, a surveyed plot, an affordable real habitat and the conservative supply/cash forecast. Approval mode creates explanatory investment proposals; automatic mode executes the same guarded quote. After real commissioning and funded owned reserve, it may raise the admission target within actual housing/jobs and the charter ceiling. It never creates a roster Kerbal. A higher approved support reserve survives ordinary reserve maintenance until the plan completes or is cancelled.

## Validation evidence and limits

Nineteen pure-domain tests cover itemization/adoption, exact quotes/replay, candidate authorization, multi-colony finite inventory/funding, saved-claim corruption, chronological cargo and atomic claims, parent cancellation, shared-fleet reorder behavior, zero-export forecasts, future cash-floor protection, external uncertainty, complete paid dependency/placement/commissioning contracts, approval/automatic growth without roster creation, higher reserve maintenance and round-robin progress. Their placement/terrain/utility witnesses are explicit test fixtures; they are not native package certificates.

Native `Expanse.WorldBridge` Release builds with zero warnings/errors. Root owns native acceptance and deployment; no live installation/save was modified in this phase. Complete actual loaded survey and unattended founding/growth remain to be proved in the isolated KSP runtime. The required registered physical stock ↔ owned stock resource loop is now implemented in [COLONY-PHYSICAL-PROCUREMENT-CONTRACTS.md](COLONY-PHYSICAL-PROCUREMENT-CONTRACTS.md): approved local deficits precede imports, with durable exact debit/credit receipts and decreasing effective purchases under the original immutable quote maximum. Observed production alone remains excluded from owned stock until that receipt.

Bounds: 64 saved plans, one active plan per colony, 64 building lines and freight loads per quote, 128 incoming claims per plan, 32 rolling growth proposals per colony, configured environment roles32/assets1024/surveys64. Existing global entities/payload/event limits still apply. Insufficient finite inventory/receiving capacity or more than64 freight loads rejects the quote instead of creating partial approvals.
