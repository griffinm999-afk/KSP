## Integration contract and authority

`ColonyPlacementScenario.Enqueue(request, out status, out reason)` accepts only stable world/colony/plot/operation IDs, certification/survey IDs, immutable SHA-256 template terms and an escrow witness supplied by the authoritative colony construction service. Templates resolve only beneath installed `GameData/ExpanseWorldBridge/Templates`; absolute, traversal and reparse paths are rejected. Contents are explicit `CraftPartId/ResourceName/Amount` allocations. All template default resource amounts are zeroed before those paid contents are allocated. The caller owns material/funds debits, escrow, crew and commissioning; the adapter never creates money, cargo or Kerbals.

The independent save-owned physical queue stages are AwaitingPlacement, Prepared, Created, Settling, Anchored, RecoveryHold and Cancelled. Construction may report operational only after Anchored **and** its separate real utility/storage/support/housing commissioning succeeds. A placement receipt cannot certify a home or throughput. Expose status reasons and the three recovery actions: `RetryUnattempted`, `ReconcileExistingBuilding`, `CancelUnstarted`. Actual/uncertain creation is never removed or refunded by these actions.

Install `package/GameData/ExpanseWorldBridge/ColonyPlacement.cfg`. Its inert per-part marker is necessary: installed stock `Part.LoadModule` skips a saved module when its prefab lacks that module. New placement explicitly rejects prefabs without the matching marker. No runtime prefab injection or third-party window suppression is used.

## Expected behavior established before native acceptance

- An equivalent operation retry returns the same receipt; changed terms under the same operation ID fail. One occupied/reserved plot cannot accept a second operation.
- No physical construction runs unless loaded flight, unpaused 1×, a stable landed reference vessel within 1,000 m, installed compatible Foundations and complete loaded terrain colliders are available.
- A certified connected fixed-root craft has fresh vessel persistent and part persistent IDs. Installed stock assembly supplies fresh vessel GUID, flight, launch and mission IDs; readback must prove their uniqueness. The active vessel must remain unchanged throughout.
- Full certified deployment footprint is sampled at at most 2 m spacing (up to 4,096 points). Actual collider envelope, slope, support gaps, neighboring collider/access clearance, zero crew and explicitly allocated contents are checked. Final body-relative settlement repeats clearance and permits at most 0.5 m horizontal root drift.
- Before anchoring, stock easing/physics hold must finish and position/angle stability must meet configured limits over at least five seconds (default ten). Anchoring cannot conceal collider intersection. Foundations must read back the exact hold/ID and applied pose within 0.01 m/0.01°.
- Missing/duplicate/changed markers after a possible assembly cause a recovery hold. An existing matching building may resume settlement; absence never permits blind respawn or refund. Occupants/reserved stock require a separate replacement/removal workflow.
- KSP save serialization stores queue, immutable requests, identities, contents, before/after hashes and anchor evidence together. `IncludedInSaveSerialization` identifies serialization, **not** independently proven disk durability. Quickload restores this scenario's saved branch; the Host is not a placement authority.
